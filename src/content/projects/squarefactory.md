I recruited the software and infrastructure engineers and built the csquare platform from scratch: microservices on Kubernetes, a GraphQL API and a Next.js frontend. In 2022 csquare became part of [SquareFactory](https://www.startupticker.ch/en/news/deeptech-startups-merge-to-offer-ai-as-a-service-experience), and we turned it into a full AutoML pipeline, from training to inference. I also isolated the on-site compute clusters behind a site-to-site VPN and a strict Google Cloud firewall.
