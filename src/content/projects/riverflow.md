Riverflow pulled activity from the main EVM chains (Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum) and routed it to webhooks, data warehouses and SaaS tools. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/30/hexa-the-startup-studio-behind-front-spendesk-and-aircall-unveils-its-next-batch-of-startups/) called it "more like Segment than Zapier". Mobula acqui-hired the team and the technology in April 2024, and I became its CTO.
