I joined in April 2024, when Mobula acqui-hired Riverflow and its engineers, and led engineering for real-time and historical market data across 80+ blockchains: a TypeScript monorepo of 40+ packages, deployed on Kubernetes on GCP, with 30+ TB in ClickHouse. Our APIs were used by [Bitpanda](https://www.bitpanda.com), [Chainlink](https://chain.link) and [Supra](https://supra.com/news/supra-partners-with-mobula/). In August 2026, after I left, fomo bought Mobula's technology and part of the team [for a reported $17M](https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/08/fomo-acquisition-mobula-on-chain-data-crypto-trading-infrastructure-web3-price/).
