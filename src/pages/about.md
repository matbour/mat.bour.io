I'm Mathieu, a CTO and founding engineer based in Metz, France. I've been shipping software since 2017 and on-chain products since 2021, usually as one of a company's first engineers. I like the stage where there is no product yet, and the one right after, when it has to hold up under real users and real money.

Today I'm CTO and late founder at FANtium, where I designed and built two crypto investment products end to end. FANtium lets fans invest in athletes and clubs: $850k in volume from 559 investors. FANstrike, a Solana launchpad for sports tokens, did $5.7M in volume and 25k trades in its first three months.

## How I work

**Security, without a security team.** Most startups I've joined had nobody in charge of security, so it became my job. I've led a CertiK audit, reviewed a Solana program pass after pass before mainnet, put treasuries behind multisigs, and rotated every secret within hours when a provider leaked. I got the FANtium job the unusual way: I reviewed their platform on my own, privately sent nine findings with proofs of concept, and they shipped the fixes in two days. Two weeks later I joined as CTO.

**Scrappy.** I fix the riskiest thing first, ship the fix in days rather than weeks, and write down the risks I decide to accept. A small control in production beats a perfect one on a roadmap.

**Facts over opinions.** I decide what to build next from funnels, metrics and revenue, and I prefer systems anyone can check. For the TheAssetsClub mint, the supply cap and sale dates were hardcoded in the contract, so nobody had to take our word for it ([the story](/posts/theassetsclub-mint/)).

**Small, senior teams.** I hire carefully, set engineering standards early, and stay hands-on across architecture, backend, frontend, smart contracts and data. From 2023 to 2025 I also taught blockchain and Solidity to engineering students at [Mewo](https://www.mewo.fr).
