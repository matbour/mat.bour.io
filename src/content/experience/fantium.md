I designed and built two products end to end: architecture, backend, frontend, smart contract integration and data.

- **FANtium:** fans invest in athletes and clubs. It has done $850k in volume, with $45k in revenue and 559 unique investors.
- **[FANstrike](https://fanstrike.fun):** a Solana launchpad for sports capital markets, with tokenization, dynamic bonding curves, vesting and DeFi-native liquidity, and partnerships with [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) and [Raydium](https://raydium.io). In its first three months: $5.7M in volume, 25k trades and 72 tokens launched.
- **Security,** with no dedicated security team: a smart contract audit that closed 3 critical vulnerabilities, multisig treasury controls, and incident response. Before FANstrike's mainnet, I ran five internal security review passes on the Solana program and tracked all 23 findings to fixed or accepted, with a written reason for every accepted risk.
